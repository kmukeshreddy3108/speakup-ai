# SpeakUp AI — 60-Second Demo & Reviewer Walkthrough Script

**Goal:** Demonstrate the core speaking loop, honest AI vs Rules labeling, feedback accuracy, notebook deduplication, and privacy controls in under 60 seconds.

---

### [00:00 - 00:10] Introduction & Setup
1. Open SpeakUp AI in the Anna App runtime.
2. Highlight the clean dark interface and live runtime status indicator (*"Anna LLM Sampling active"* or *"Rule-based basic mode"*).
3. Select **Job Interview** scenario, pick **10m** duration, and tap **"Start Speaking Practice"**.

### [00:10 - 00:25] Spoken Practice & Real-Time Audio
1. The coach greets you and asks the interview opening question. Notice the speaking bars and clear audio output.
2. Tap the center microphone button. Speak a sample answer with a deliberate grammar point:
   > *"I have 20 years, and I am very exiting to join your engineering team."*
3. Tap **"Finish & Send"** (or use the typed fallback).

### [00:25 - 00:40] Constructive Feedback & Contextual Dialogue
1. Observe the Coach prompt: it acknowledges your message and asks a relevant follow-up question using the STAR method without any repetitive sentences.
2. Point out the **Coach Label**: clearly marked as *"AI Coach"* (or *"Coach (basic mode)"* if sampling is inactive).
3. Inspect the **Feedback Deck**:
   - Catches *"I have 20 years"* → *"I am 20 years old"*.
   - Catches *"exiting"* → *"excited"*.
   - Shows 2 natural alternatives and the **"Try rewriting this"** button.

### [00:40 - 00:50] Mistake Notebook & Deduplication
1. Switch to the **Notebook** tab.
2. Point out the deduplicated entry with a frequency counter (`Seen 1x`), clear rule explanations, and filter buttons (*All, New, Practising, Mastered*).
3. Tap **"Mark Mastered"** and show the status update.

### [00:50 - 01:00] Real Progress & Privacy
1. Switch to the **Progress** tab: highlight real numbers (turns completed, words spoken, streak). Explain that all numbers derive strictly from real data with zero invented CEFR scores.
2. Tap **"Privacy"** in the header to show the Data Export (JSON) and Delete All Data capabilities.
3. Finish the session: show the summary screen with strengths and scenario phrases.
