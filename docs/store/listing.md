## Full Description
SpeakUp AI is your dedicated English conversation coach built natively for the Anna platform. Unlike generic chatbots, SpeakUp AI provides an interactive, voice-first speaking stage designed to overcome hesitation and build conversational fluency.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Guided Practice Scenarios:** Choose from Self-Introduction, Job Interview (STAR method), College Discussion (academic debate), or Everyday Casual Conversation.
- **Honest AI vs Rule-Based Coaching:** Powered by Anna's native LLM Sampling when available on your plan, with seamless fallback to contextual rule-based practice mode. Every response truthfully indicates its source ("AI Coach" or "Coach (basic mode)").
- **Agent Session Integration:** Multi-turn streaming conversations powered by Anna Agent Sessions for natural, context-aware dialogue with cancel and recovery support.
- **Non-Repetitive Dialogue:** Intelligent conversation management prevents repetitive questions and nudges short answers into expanded thoughts.
- **Accurate Feedback & Better Expressions:** Distinguishes spoken transcripts from typed text, eliminating false alerts on speech-recognition capitalization. Provides up to three natural alternatives and an interactive "Try rewriting" workflow.
- **Mistake Notebook:** Automatically saves grammar and phrasing items with deduplication, status tracking (New, Practising, Mastered), search, and deletion.
- **Honest Progress Tracking:** Metrics (sessions, practice minutes, words spoken, words per turn, mastered notebook items) are computed solely from your practice history — with zero speculative CEFR ratings or invented accuracy scores.
- **Privacy First:** All data is saved exclusively to your personal Anna Persistent Storage (APS KV) partition. You can export your data as JSON or delete all records at any time.

## Requirements
- Built for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge) supporting the Web Speech API and SpeechSynthesis.
- Full typed-text fallback available for all platforms and silent practice environments.
