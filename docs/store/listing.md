## Full Description
SpeakUp AI is your dedicated English conversation coach built natively for the Anna platform. Unlike generic chatbots, SpeakUp AI provides an interactive, voice-first speaking stage designed to overcome hesitation and build conversational fluency.

### Key Capabilities:
- **12 Real-Life Practice Scenarios:** Practise everyday conversation, workplace stand-ups, STAR job interviews, academic debates, ordering food, customer service calls, travel check-ins, storytelling, and daily news.
- **True Hands-Free Mode:** Practise speaking while walking or doing chores with automatic silence detection, wake lock, media session controls, and voice commands ("Pause", "Repeat", "Slower", "End session").
- **Honest AI vs Rule-Based Coaching:** Powered by multi-turn Anna Agent Sessions for natural dialogue with seamless fallback to contextual rule-based practice mode. Every response truthfully indicates its source ("AI Coach" or "Coach (basic mode)").
- **Progressive Learner Profile:** Remembers your speaking goals, CEFR level, and topics of interest across sessions without repeating onboarding questions, with strict privacy protection against sensitive data.
- **Accurate Feedback & Better Expressions:** Distinguishes spoken transcripts from typed text, eliminating false alerts on speech-recognition capitalization. Provides natural alternatives and an interactive "Try rewriting" workflow.
- **Mistake Notebook & Spaced Repetition:** Automatically saves grammar and phrasing items with deduplication, status tracking (New, Practising, Mastered), search, and interactive spaced-repetition challenge review.
- **Honest Progress Tracking:** Metrics (sessions, practice minutes, words spoken, words per turn, mastered notebook items) are computed solely from your practice history — with zero speculative CEFR ratings or invented accuracy scores.
- **Privacy First:** All data is saved exclusively to your personal Anna Persistent Storage (APS KV) partition. You can export your data as JSON or delete all records at any time.

## Requirements
- Built for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge) supporting the Web Speech API and SpeechSynthesis.
- Full typed-text fallback available for all platforms and silent practice environments.
