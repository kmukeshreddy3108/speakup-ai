⚙️ Settings & Privacy Transparency

Data Storage & Scoping

Your notebook entries and progress counters are stored securely in your Anna Persistent Storage (APS KV) tool partition. All data is scoped to your Anna account. No external database, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

AI Coaching vs Basic Mode

When your Anna plan supports LLM Sampling, the coach requests completions from Anna's native host LLM (reverse Sampling). If Sampling is unavailable or denied, the app automatically runs in deterministic, rule-based basic mode. We never present rule-based responses as AI output.

Data Rights & Control

You have full control over your stored practice records. You may export your entire notebook and progress as JSON or delete all records at any time.
