# SpeakUp AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What the app stores: your practice sessions, answers, saved mistakes, vocabulary, and progress statistics. [State exactly where: Anna Storage and/or your browser's local storage.]

What is sent to others: when Anna's AI is available, the text of your current answer and minimal conversation context are sent to Anna's language model to generate coaching. If it isn't available, the app uses rule-based checks and nothing is sent for coaching.

Voice input: if you use the microphone, speech recognition is handled by your browser, and audio may be processed by your browser vendor's service (for example Google in Chrome). SpeakUp AI does not store audio.

What we don't do: no ads, no selling data, no third-party analytics, no accounts.

Your control: export all data or delete all data from Settings at any time.

Children: the app is intended for learners of general audiences; do not enter personal information you wouldn't want processed as described above.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/kmukeshreddy3108/speakup-ai/issues
